HONORING TRADITION, EMPOWERING PROGRESS

Based in Addis Ababa, TESHOME GABRE-MARIAM BOKAN METASEBIA provides a full range of legal and consultancy services in connection with corporate, business, and investments in Ethiopia. Recently, we have expanded our offerings to include tax and financial consultancy services. This allows our clients to benefit from a comprehensive, one-stop compliance solution covering corporate governance, taxation, and financial advisory needs.

Legal services are rendered by Ms. Wossen Bokan and other lawyers retained by the Firm, all individually registered and licensed in accordance with the laws of Ethiopia.

This Consultancy Firm is named after Mr. Teshome Gabre-Mariam Bokan under whom the Managing Director, Wossen Bokan, has been trained as a lawyer for over a decade. Teshome was a leading and internationally recognised Ethiopian lawyer who also happened to be the first Attorney General of Ethiopia under Emperor Haile-Selassie. Teshome was a prominent figure and passionate lover of Ethiopia. He is also the father of the Managing Director.

Meet Our Team

Our Lawyers

WOSSEN TESHOME BOKAN

Managing Director

Wossen Teshome Bokan is a leading intellectual property and corporate lawyer with a law degree from the University of London and over 25 years of experience in the field. Wossen has also been trained under her late father, Mr. Teshome Gabre-Mariam Bokan, and worked with him for 13 years both as a corporate lawyer and leading his IP department. In 2012, she established her own boutique intellectual property firm which she has then merged with her father's firm, after his passing at the end of 2016.

TAMRAT TALEGET TESSEMA

Senior Attorney

Tamrat Talegeta Tessema (PhD) is an Assistant Professor of Law of Arsi University in Ethiopia and practices corporate law in general. His main practice areas include Merger and Acquisition, litigation, tax administration, banking and insurance. Tamrat holds a PhD from Seoul National University, with an LLB and LLM from Addis Ababa University.

TILAHUN MITIKU TESFAYE

SENIOR Litigation Lawyer

Tilahun Mitiku Tesfaye is the firm's leading litigation lawyer with an LLB and LLM from Addis Ababa University. Tilahun served as a presiding judge in civil, commercial, labour and penal divisions of the Federal Court of Ethiopia before he started private practice over 20 years ago. Other than litigation, Tilahun is also an astute corporate lawyer with deep understanding of commercial principles and practical implications of legal advice.

GEMECHU MERERA FANA

Corporate Lawyer

Gemechu Merera Fana is the firm's leading corporate lawyer, recognized for his deep expertise in corporate transactions, litigation, and regulatory matters. Prior to starting private practice, Gemechu served as a public prosecutor and a judge at the Federal Tax Appeal Commission, bringing valuable government and judicial experience to his corporate advisory work.

RUTH GIZACHEW GUYASA

Ruth Gizachew Guyasa is a consultant handling and overseeing assignments in corporate, tax, mergers and acquisition matters of the Firm. DANIEL TARIKU TAMIRU

TIGIST DANCHA TENECHA
