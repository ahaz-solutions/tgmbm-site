Main Areas of Practice

The Firm practices in areas such as international finance, joint ventures, commercial transactions, construction, investment, natural resources and energy, intellectual property, employment law, and international arbitration and local litigation.

The Firm's well-developed practice has represented international organisations and corporate entities. We assist companies in all facets of corporate activities from formation of a company under local laws to advising corporate clients on labour, tax, banking and related corporate issues. We also assist in winding-up and deregistration of those companies that wish not to operate in Ethiopia anymore. For this, we have a selected team of lawyers each specialising in the relevant laws of Ethiopia.

We further provide services in aviation matters particularly with foreign financiers and leasing companies where various entities have leased or bought aircraft, aircraft engines and other spare parts advising on lease, loan and security documents and assisting in the registration of aircraft and other relevant services with the Ethiopian Civil Aviation Authority.
