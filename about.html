Our Misssion

Our firm is built on a foundation of legal excellence and deep understanding of Ethiopian business landscapes.

Teshome Gabre-Mariam Bokan Metasebia is a leading consultancy firm based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, specialising in business and investment advisory services for both new entrants in the Ethiopian market. For businesses already operating in Ethiopia, we offer a full suite of legal, tax, and accounting consultancy services tailored to local regulations and industry standards.

Our firm is grounded in a legacy of legal excellence, inspired by the work of the late Mr. Teshome Gabre-Mariam Bokan, a renowned Ethiopian legal figure whose vision continues to guide our commitment to legal integrity and professional services.

We are proud to carry forward the legacy of Mr. Teshome Gabre-Mariam Bokan, a leading and internationally recognised Ethiopian lawyer, whose expertise and integrity sets the standard of services provided by our firm.
